General feedback

Here is some feedback on the writing task. These are useful points to check as you prepare your next text.

Follow the task

Describe and compare your style and another person's. Include the situations and shopping details requested in the task. When you contrast two people, make it clear who you are talking about.

Check agreement

Articles and adjectives need to match the noun. Look carefully at both gender and number, especially with clothes and colours.

Look at the verb

Check who does the action and choose the right present-tense form. Remember that gustar works differently: it agrees with the thing or activity you like.

Connect your ideas

Group related ideas and use familiar connectors such as y, pero, porque, por ejemplo and por último. Try not to repeat the same sentence pattern too often.

What came up most often? Gender and number agreement were especially common, including agreement with colours and clothes. Gustar and encantar, particularly the choice of pronoun and verb form, also caused difficulties in several texts. When comparing two people, remember to make both subject pronouns explicit, as requested in this task.

Keep practising! Making mistakes is part of learning. Use your individual feedback and these examples to spot patterns, correct your own text and build confidence.

Common errors to look out for

The examples below come from your class texts; some are shortened or anonymised. The red text shows the part to change; the green text shows one possible correction.

G · N · GN

Gender and number

Articles and adjectives agree with the noun. Ropa is usually singular; pantalones and zapatillas are plural.

una camiseta blanco → una camiseta blanca
unos vaqueros azul → unos vaqueros azules
mucho ropas → mucha ropa
G · N

Colours and patterns

Many colour adjectives change to match the item. Some, such as gris, have the same form for masculine and feminine but change in the plural. Estampada is an adjective describing ropa.

ropa negro → ropa negra
unas zapatillas gris → unas zapatillas grises
ropa con colores diferentes y estampada
V · GR

Gustar and encantar

Use gusta / encanta with a singular thing or an infinitive, and gustan / encantan with plural things. The pronoun shows who likes them.

me gustan más comprar → me gusta más comprar
nos gusta unas zapatillas → nos gustan unas zapatillas
le encantan llevar camisetas → le encanta llevar camisetas
MW · GR

Who likes it?

When you name the person, use a and keep the pronoun me / le. With a mí, use me.

mi mejor amiga le gusta ropa → a mi mejor amiga le gusta ropa
a mi amiga le gusta la marca
a mí le gusta → a mí me gusta
V

Present tense and infinitives

Match the verb to the person: yo prefiero, ella prefiere, nosotras vamos. After gustar or encantar, use an infinitive for the next action.

yo prefieres → yo prefiero
me gusta llevo la chaqueta → me gusta llevar la chaqueta
le gusta lleva → le gusta llevar
PRO

Make the contrast clear

For this task, include the subject pronouns explicitly when contrasting the two people. This makes it easy to follow whose style you are describing.

Yo prefiero llevar ropa sencilla y elegante, pero él prefiere ropa aburrida y fea.
EXP · VOC

Useful expressions

Learn useful combinations as short phrases. Check prepositions and vocabulary when you are unsure.

ir al compras → ir de compras
ocasiones formal → ocasiones formales
por la última → por último
SP

Spelling and accents

Check the spelling of familiar words and accents. Mi means “my”, but a mí means “to me”; el is “the”, but él means “he”.

para mi → para mí
el compra ropa → él compra ropa
también · último · pantalones
